What you actually get

Built for the bench, not a generic sports CRM

Video snippet creator

Open a team video, cut the clip you care about in seconds, and mark it up before it hits the library. Overlays cover arrows and place-and-speak notes. Add tags, mute the game audio when you need to, and save the snippet.

Library you can actually find

Search the video library and filter by tag so “PP, left-side entry” does not disappear into last month’s folder.

Assign to players

Send a snippet with an email link. Then check whether the player opened it and how much watch time they put in — useful before you ask “did you see the weak-side winger?”

Player goals

Keep development tight: each player can have up to three active goals, so the conversation stays on a few habits instead of a laundry list.

Team whiteboard

White ice plus a rink. Drop text, X, and O pins, and add an optional link on a pin when the diagram should point at a clip or a note.

Notes, roster, staff

Coach notes stay personal for assistants; the head coach sees the team’s notes. Head coaches add players and assistant coaches on the roster.
